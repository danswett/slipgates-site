What Slipgates handles

Slipgates connects the coding sessions on your PC to your own devices. Normal use sends prompts, replies and approvals between those devices, encrypted end to end so the relay cannot read them. That content does leave your PC to reach your phone. The relay handles connection details, including which devices are online, when they connect and how much they send.

Optional diagnostics

Optional automatic diagnostics are not enabled in this beta and cannot be turned on yet. Their design excludes session content; they must pass technical and privacy reviews before any uploads are enabled. Any future optional diagnostics will require your separate opt-in. You can use Slipgates without agreeing to them.

Getting help

For help today, reply privately in the email or Teams conversation your invitation came in. The support team can read what you send. Send only the minimum needed. Remove names, paths, prompts, code, tokens and sign-in codes; review any screenshot or log before sending it. Do not post support content or personal information in public GitHub issues. We are not promising an in-app report preview in this build.

Your requests

For privacy questions, or to request access, correction or deletion of your information, email support@slipgates.com. Requests will be handled as required by applicable law. If optional diagnostics become available, you can refuse them or later turn them off without losing normal functionality. Turning them off stops future optional diagnostic collection and uploads; it does not itself delete events already received. Contact us separately about deleting retained information.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling does not itself erase server or support records. The Windows profile, including device keys, settings and logs, also remains locally unless you separately remove it as described in the uninstall instructions.

This website

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from other sites.
